# Project Statement — Billora

## Problem Statement

Small shops often need a simple way to manage customer details, product details, discounts, billing calculations, and previous bills without doing all the calculations manually.

Billora was developed as a simple command-line billing system to make this process more organized and reduce calculation errors during billing.

## Scope

Billora is designed for small shops that need a basic and organized billing system.

The project covers:

- Shop information management
- Customer details
- Product and quantity entry
- Discount calculation
- Bill generation
- Bill history
- JSON-based storage
- Basic input validation

The current version is designed to run through the command line.

## Target Users

The main target users of Billora are:

- Small shopkeepers
- Retail shop owners
- Store staff who handle billing
- Students or beginners who want to understand how a modular billing system works

## High-Level Features

1. **Shop Management**
   - Store and view shop name, address, and license number.

2. **Customer and Product Management**
   - Enter customer details and add one or more products to a bill.

3. **Billing and Calculation**
   - Calculate discounts, final prices, total amount, and total savings.

4. **Receipt Generation**
   - Display a properly formatted bill receipt with shop, customer, and product details.

5. **Bill History**
   - Save completed bills and view previous billing records.

6. **Input Validation**
   - Check important inputs such as quantity, MRP, discount percentage, customer name, and mobile number.
